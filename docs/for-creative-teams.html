For creative teams

Run an AI-native studio without losing the craft

Your internal studio turns out briefs, concepts, and assets at volume — and every project starts from a blank page, even though the persona, the brand, and the last campaign's learnings all already exist somewhere. OSMM structures the creative decision layer so your studio, and the AI tools inside it, start from your real context instead of a re-paste.

The problem

The context exists. It's just scattered.

The persona is in a research deck. The brand is in a PDF. The last campaign's what-worked is in someone's head. So every new brief starts by reassembling context that already exists — and the quality of the output swings with whoever happened to write the brief that day.

AI tools promise to speed this up, and they do help — but you re-paste the same four documents into every session, and the results are only as good as that ad-hoc context. Without structure, an AI-augmented studio is just a faster way to produce inconsistent work.

What OSMM does

Structure the inputs; keep the craft

OSMM turns the creative decision layer — briefs, personas, journeys, creative strategy — into objects your studio and its AI tools reuse. Build the context once; every brief, concept, and asset starts from it instead of from nothing. The insight, the idea, and the art direction stay unmistakably yours. What changes is that the inputs are finally reliable.

Ask for a creative brief and the composer pulls your brand, persona, and offer objects, and drafts one grounded in all of them — a first draft you tailor, not a blank page you fill. Structure once, compose forever.
